**Causal inference and experiments.** Randomized survey experiments, instrumental variables, power analysis, and weighted, clustered estimation.

**Building data from scratch.** As data and project manager for the NSF-funded Charters of Intergovernmental Organizations project, I managed a team of research assistants building a dataset of roughly 20,000 observations, and I cleaned and managed the data in R.

**Text as data.** A Malyi Center–funded project tracing the origins of election denialism through town hall transcripts and politicians' speeches.

**Communicating results.** Peer-reviewed publications, including in *Democratization*, and two years as a full-time teaching assistant in political science at the University of Chicago, including research methods.
